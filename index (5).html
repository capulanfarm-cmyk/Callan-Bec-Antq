From Agricultural Waste to Community Wealth

Welcome to Callan Basic Ecclesial Community (BEC) Livelihood Association. We upcycle raw, discarded local coconut husks and shells into high-performing, certified organic garden setups.

Explore Product Line

Our Vision

To establish a model community-led social enterprise in Sebaste, Antique that elevates provincial Karay-a craftsmanship while championing environmental restoration and zero-waste farming systems.

Our Mission

To empower small-scale farmers, seniors, and weavers across Barangay Callan by producing top-tier, bio-safe, chemical-free structural plant supports and growth mediums supported directly by the DTI and PCA.
